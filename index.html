How to play

1. Roll Team. You get one IPL franchise + season and the entire squad from that team-season.

2. Click a player. Then click the batting slot where you want them. The role guide is Top Order 1–3, Middle Order 4–7, and Lower Order 8–11.

3. After you pick a player, roll again for the next round. You cannot reroll the current team until you make a pick, so you cannot keep rerolling for better squads.

Ratings: season-performance is the main factor, with a small reputation component. For 2026, batting ratings use runs, average and strike rate; bowling ratings use wickets, economy, workload and innings. A 15% reputation blend prevents one poor season from completely erasing an established player’s quality, while the season still drives 85% of the rating. Older seasons are conservatively calibrated from the historical season database. 95+ is reserved for extraordinary seasons; 90-94 is rare elite territory. 80-85 means good, 75-79 means okay, and 70-74 means poor.

The structure is inspired by the roll → draft → XI-building loop of 7a0 and 38-0. Ratings prioritize what the player actually did in that IPL season, not their career reputation.
